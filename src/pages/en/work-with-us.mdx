## Work with us

Four things people write to us about. Pick the one that sounds like you and it goes straight to the right person,
with your reason already attached.

<section id="private-events" class="scroll-mt-32 space-y-4">
    <span class="text-xs font-semibold uppercase tracking-[0.42em] text-neutral-400">Private shows</span>
    <h2>Book the club for your own night</h2>
    <p>Weddings, birthdays, company parties and brand weekends. We have an 80-seat room in the centre of Playa del Carmen, and we book the comedians, run the show in English or Spanish, and handle the bar. Tell us the date, roughly how many people, and what the night is for. A week or more of notice gets you the best line-up and the best price.</p>

One night, a season, or a regular slot.</p>
