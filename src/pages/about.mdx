## Why Riviera audiences needed bilingual punchlines long before TikTok comedians boarded flights.

Iguana started as stubborn curiosity: travellers kept asking concierge desks for comedy beyond karaoke, while locals politely endured half-translated trivia nights pretending to laugh. Nobody was programming English stand-up with the same obsessive rigor we saw elsewhere in México.

We mapped rooms nobody thought could stay quiet longer than eighteen minutes—hotel ballrooms repurposed Wednesdays, cenote-town cafés persuaded to darken early, Playa rooftops risking squalls for punchlines nobody wanted drowned out.

## Touring comics reroute layovers deliberately now.

Guests fly in chasing punchlines louder than cenote selfies. Hospitality partners lean on our producing desk so staff never improvise ticketing math at midnight—every guest hears the joke at the advertised door time with microphones behaving.
